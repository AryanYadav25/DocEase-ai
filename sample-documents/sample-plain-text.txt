1. Purpose
These Membership Terms explain how residents of Riverside can borrow books, use study rooms, and access digital resources at the Riverside Community Library (the "Library").

2. Eligibility
Any person aged 12 or older who lives, works or studies in the Riverside district may apply for a membership card. Applicants under 18 need written consent from a parent or guardian.

3. Borrowing limits
A standard member may borrow up to 8 items at a time for a loan period of 21 days. Reference materials, periodicals and rare books cannot be removed from the premises.

4. Late returns
Items returned after the due date incur a late fee of 0.50 per item per day, capped at 15.00 per item. Members with outstanding fees above 20.00 will have borrowing privileges suspended until the balance is cleared.

5. Renewals
Items may be renewed twice online or at the front desk unless another member has placed a hold on them. Renewal requests must be made before the due date, 31 December 2026 being the last day of the current membership year.

6. Study rooms
Study rooms can be booked for up to 3 hours per day. Bookings must be made at least 24 hours in advance through the online portal. A room that is not occupied within 15 minutes of the booking start time will be released.

7. Lost or damaged items
Members are responsible for items borrowed on their card. The replacement cost of a lost or damaged item is the current retail price plus a 5.00 processing fee.

8. Data protection
The Library stores member names, contact details and borrowing history for the purpose of running the service. Records are deleted 24 months after a membership lapses. Members may request a copy of their data by writing to the Library Manager.

9. Changes to these terms
The Library Board reviews these terms annually. Material changes will be announced on the Library website at least 30 days before they take effect.
